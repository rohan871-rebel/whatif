GHOST SIGNAL — WHAT IF?

Test the reliability of AI when patient data cannot be trusted.

⚠️ Research demonstration only. Not for diagnosis, triage, treatment, or real-patient decisions. Default cohort marked SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — NOT CLINICAL EVIDENCE.

Executive Research Summary

Continuous hospital telemetry streams (heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure, respiratory rate, and core temperature) feed clinical AI risk models. In real high-acuity environments, loose sensors, motion artifacts, ambient light interference, and stale edge buffers produce ghost signals that trigger false alarms or silently mask acute deterioration.

Core Platform Capabilities

  • Data-Quality Monitor: Real-time detection of missing values, timestamp staleness (>120m), and implausible readings using configurable research rules.
  • WHAT IF Simulator: Injects controlled Gaussian noise (0–100%), simulates sensor dropouts (e.g. pulse ox probe detachment), and models timestamp drift to quantify model risk score migration.
  • Model Comparison: Evaluates Baseline Random Forest (all eligible features) vs Genetic Algorithm (GA) feature-selection Random Forest on untouched held-out test records.
  • Explainability & Attribution: Plain-language narratives explaining input perturbations without implying biological causation.
  • SDG Alignment: Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals 3 (Target 3.8: Safe Digital Health) and 9 (Target 9.5: Resilient Infrastructure).
